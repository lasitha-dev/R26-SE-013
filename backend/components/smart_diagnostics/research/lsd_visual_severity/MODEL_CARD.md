# LSD visual-grade prototype model card

Version: initial isolated experiment, completed 15 October 2026, Asia/Colombo. The trained local export is [artifacts/weights/best_model.pt](artifacts/weights/best_model.pt), approximately 6.21 MB. It is ignored by Git. Its SHA256 is recorded in [artifacts/metrics.json](artifacts/metrics.json). See the [experiment report](EXPERIMENT_REPORT.md) for the complete audit, selection process, uncertainty, and grouping corrections.

**Intended use:** supervised research and a project demonstration of inherited LSD visual skin-lesion grades on cattle photographs similar to the processed DataHub collection. The output is `visual_lsd_lesion_grade`, with class order `normal`, `mild`, `severe`. These are the training dataset's image categories. Normal does not confirm a clinically healthy animal. This classifier does not confirm LSD or distinguish it from other skin conditions.

**Clinical boundary:** the checkpoint explicitly sets `clinical_severity_supported=false` and `clinical_stage_supported=false`. The research CLI returns null for clinical severity and stage and requires veterinary review. Clinical grading, prognosis, treatment, urgency, lesion age, infectiousness, and whole-animal lesion coverage were not learned or validated. No changes were made to the application pipeline or veterinary records.

**Architecture and inputs:** torchvision MobileNet V3 Small with ImageNet1K V1 initialization and a three-logit neural head. Fine tuning updated the classifier and last four encoder blocks. Input is an RGB photograph, EXIF oriented, centered in a square with padding value `(114,114,114)`, resized to 224 x 224, converted to `[0,1]`, and normalized with mean `[0.485,0.456,0.406]`, standard deviation `[0.229,0.224,0.225]`. Inference applies softmax after dividing logits by temperature 1.07696. Model probabilities are not clinical confidence estimates. There is no pixel-mask output.

**Training data:** [LumpySkinDisease_DataHub v1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11003967), unbalanced processed release. From 1,019 decoded images, the documented audit retained 893 photographs: 636 normal, 184 mild, 73 severe. Content review excluded non-photographs/figures and related unsuitable content, conflicting-label duplicate components, and two exact duplicates. The audit was performed by an assistant, without veterinary regrading. See [content_review.json](content_review.json), [manifest.csv](artifacts/manifest.csv), and [group_review_errata.json](artifacts/group_review_errata.json).

**Evaluation separation:** 527 training, 177 validation, and 189 test images were assigned using conservative image-group bundles before training augmentation. Known source matches stay within partitions. Eight manual constraints were later found overly broad; they contain only same-label images and introduce no cross-partition leakage. The frozen split and reported model were preserved. Corrected matching-source membership checks are recorded separately. Animal/farm independence, hidden duplicate independence, and clinical label correctness have not been established.

**Selection and results:** validation macro F1 selected epoch 8 of the fine-tuned model over a balanced frozen-feature logistic-regression baseline. Training stopped after 13 epochs. Validation accuracy was 85.88%, macro F1 0.7883; validation severe recall was 62.5% on 16 images. On the initially held-out test partition, accuracy was 90.48%, balanced accuracy 87.84%, macro F1 0.8775, quadratic weighted kappa 0.8847, and ordinal MAE 0.0952. Severe recall was 15/17 (88.24%) across ten frozen severe bundles, with an empirical 95% bundle-bootstrap interval of 64.2% to 100%. Always-normal test accuracy was 70.37%. These are internal inherited-label metrics, not external clinical validation.

**Known limitations:** the source includes stock-style/watermarked photographs and mixed capture conditions, with no source-by-image clinical provenance, diagnostic confirmation, case-level examinations, or independently supplied reviewer grades. Content filtering and source grouping are incomplete and fallible. The model can learn background, breed, framing, watermark, or source cues. It always returns probabilities, including for drawings, other diseases, non-cattle inputs, or poor-quality photographs. It has no validated rejection or unassessable mechanism. It was not evaluated on the application's YOLO-selected ROIs, on an independent farm cohort, or for FMD/mastitis. A small test set, especially severe cases, makes estimates unstable.

**Runtime checks:** all 189 saved-checkpoint CPU predictions matched recorded GPU class decisions; maximum probability difference was approximately 0.001953. The three class examples also passed CLI checks for the veterinary boundary. Eight focused tests cover grouping, split integrity, preprocessing, and checkpoint compatibility. [artifacts/environment.json](artifacts/environment.json) records Python/package versions, and [artifacts/run_protocol.json](artifacts/run_protocol.json) records training configuration. Training used an RTX 3050 Laptop GPU; the CLI defaults to CPU and requires no running API service.

**Data/weight provenance:** the Zenodo record declares CC BY 4.0 and attributes the dataset to Francesco Branda. Third-party watermarked content is present, so individual image provenance/rights were not established solely by that record. Source images and contact sheets remain local. The official ImageNet backbone URL and hash are recorded in [artifacts/backbone_provenance.json](artifacts/backbone_provenance.json). Dataset checksums and source notebook evidence are retained alongside the experiment. The project does not claim a verified disease-specific pretrained severity checkpoint from the dataset authors; this checkpoint was trained locally.

**Next use decision:** proceed with a clearly labeled research demo; keep veterinary clinical grading. Before application integration, obtain external, vet-reviewed cases with animal/farm separation, disease confirmation/negative look-alikes, explicit visual grading criteria, capture guidance, and a frozen evaluation. Introduce an unassessable path and evaluate the actual application crops. Broader clinical grading needs matched clinical observations and its own validation.
